Update Keepiq on your server

This extension version needs a newer Keepiq on your Nextcloud server. Ask your administrator to update it, then open the extension again.

Connect to Keepiq

Use a Nextcloud app password (Settings → Security), not your login password. Disconnect deletes it again.

Unlock vault

Your master password is used only in this extension to decrypt your vault. It never reaches the server.

Approve from another device

Open Keepiq on a device where your vault is unlocked and approve this request there. Check that it shows these same words.
