Passkey in the field's suggestions

Tests signing in with a passkey straight from the username field, without a separate Keepiq window. It ends on the signed-in page, the same as signing in with the Sign in button.

  1. Register a passkey for this site on the Passkey page first.
  2. Make sure Keepiq is unlocked and "Show autofill suggestions on form fields" is on in its settings.
  3. Come back here, click the Username field and pick your passkey in the list.

What to expect: Keepiq's suggestion list opens under the field with your passkey above any logins, named after the login and the user name, for example "localhost, alice". Pick it and you land on the signed-in page at once, like the Sign in button takes you there, and it says you signed in with a passkey and as whom. If something goes wrong, you stay here and the reason shows under the form. If Keepiq is locked, the list shows no passkeys until you unlock it. If this browser cannot do this, the page says so.
