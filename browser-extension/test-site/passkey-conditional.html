Passkey in the field's suggestions

Tests signing in with a passkey straight from the username field, without a separate Keepiq window.

  1. Register a passkey for this site on the Passkey page first.
  2. Make sure Keepiq is unlocked and "Show autofill suggestions on form fields" is on in its settings.
  3. Come back here and click the Username field.

What to expect: Keepiq's suggestion list opens under the field with your passkey above any logins, named after the login and the user name, for example "localhost, alice". Pick it and you are signed in at once, and the result shows under the form. If Keepiq is locked, the list shows no passkeys until you unlock it. If this browser cannot do this, the page says so.
