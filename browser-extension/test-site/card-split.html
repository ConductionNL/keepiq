Card in split fields

Fields without tokens that each take part of a member. A card with number 4111 1111 1111 1111 and expiry 03/27 fills the card type with "VISA", the four boxes with "4111" and "1111" in order, and the selects with "03" and "2027".

Combined expiry

One expiry field asking for a four-digit year: it takes "03 / 2027".
