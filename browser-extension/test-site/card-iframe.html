Card fields in frames

"Name on card" is in this page, the card fields in frames, as with Stripe or Adyen. Fill a card from the list on "Name on card": Keepiq asks "Also fill on 127.0.0.1?" first. "Fill" fills every frame, "Only localhost" fills this page and the same-site frame, and "Cancel" fills nothing.

Same-site frame

On port 8101 of this host: the same site, so it takes part without a question.

Cross-site frame

On 127.0.0.1, another site: it takes part only after the question.

Frame added later

Press the button, then pick a card within 5 seconds and wait for the new frame before you answer: it is on ads.localhost, which the question did not name, so "Fill" asks again, naming it, and fills nothing before that answer.
